CookSync - Privacy Policy

Last updated: September 2, 2026

CookSync is designed as an offline-first Android application. Data created in the app remains on the user's device and is not sent to servers operated by Nuvement.

1. Controller and contact

CookSync is published under the Nuvement brand. The contact person and controller for any processing described in this policy is Antonio Cirillo, Italy.

For privacy-related requests: nuvement.apps@gmail.com.

2. How CookSync works

CookSync coordinates the preparation of multiple dishes through local scheduling, cooking sessions, countdowns and Android notifications. The current version does not require an account and does not use a Nuvement backend or Nuvement cloud services for the app to operate.

3. Data stored on the device

Information entered or generated by the user, such as dishes, preparation steps, meals, kitchen configuration, schedules, sessions and history, is stored locally in the app's private storage, primarily in a SQLite database.

This data may include information freely chosen by the user in names and descriptions. Nuvement does not receive this content and cannot access it remotely.

4. Collection, transmission and sharing

CookSync does not use user accounts, analytics, advertising, profiling systems, artificial intelligence services, in-app purchases or Nuvement cloud services. In the current implementation, the app does not transmit personal data, user-created content or usage data to Nuvement, and does not share such data with third parties for advertising or analytics purposes.

The app uses third-party software components for local technical functions such as database storage, notifications, timezone handling and app version information. CookSync does not use these components to send user data to analytics or advertising services.

5. Notifications and Android permissions

CookSync may use local notifications to remind the user when cooking activities start or end. Depending on the Android version, the app may request or use the following system permissions or access:

  • Notifications, to display cooking alerts;
  • Exact alarms, when allowed by the user, to make scheduled alerts more precise;
  • Boot completed, to allow Android to restore scheduled notifications correctly after the device restarts.

CookSync also reads the timezone configured on the device so that local times can be scheduled correctly. These operations take place on the device. Denying notification-related permissions does not prevent the Cooking Mode from working; notification behavior degrades gracefully when necessary.

6. Backup and device-to-device transfer

CookSync does not support automatic cloud backup of application data or device-to-device transfer of its data. The Android configuration explicitly excludes application databases, preferences and files from the backup and transfer features managed by the operating system.

As a result, CookSync data remains tied to the installation on the device and automatic portability to another device is not promised.

7. Retention and deletion of local data

Local data remains on the device until the user deletes it through available app features, clears the app's data in Android settings, or uninstalls CookSync. Because Nuvement does not hold a remote copy of local data, Nuvement cannot recover or delete it remotely.

8. Voluntary email communications

If the user voluntarily contacts Nuvement at the email address provided in this policy, Nuvement receives the information contained in the message, such as the sender's email address, any name used and the content of the request. This information is used only to handle and respond to the communication and is not collected by the CookSync app.

Depending on the context, this processing is based on Nuvement's legitimate interest in handling and responding to communications, on steps requested by the user before entering into a contract, or on compliance with applicable legal obligations.

Correspondence may be retained for the period reasonably necessary to handle the request and to comply with any legal obligations. The email service used to receive and send messages may process technical data under its own terms and privacy policy.

9. Rights and control over data

For data stored exclusively on the device, control remains with the user through CookSync and Android features. Nuvement cannot access this data remotely.

For personal data provided directly to Nuvement, users may exercise their applicable data protection rights by contacting Nuvement using the address provided above. Users also have the right to lodge a complaint with the competent supervisory authority.

10. External services and platforms

Distribution and installation of the app may involve Google Play and Android services on the device. These services are provided by third parties and operate under their own policies and terms. This policy describes the behavior of CookSync and services directly operated by Nuvement.

11. Changes to this policy

This policy may be updated when the app's features, data practices or applicable requirements change. The published version will always show the date of the latest update.
