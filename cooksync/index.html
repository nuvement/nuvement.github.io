CookSync

Organize cooking times so every dish is ready together. CookSync coordinates dishes, preparation steps and kitchen resources around the moment you want to serve.

Get CookSync on Google Play

About

Less timing in your head. More attention on what you are cooking.

Preparing several dishes at the same time can get complicated: different timings, occupied appliances, steps that depend on each other, and dishes that finish too early or too late. CookSync builds a coordinated cooking plan and guides you through it step by step.

What it does

Plan around serving time

Start now, start at a chosen time, or work backward from when you want the meal to be ready.

Coordinate multiple dishes

CookSync accounts for active and passive steps, dependencies and the attention of a single cook.

Manage kitchen resources

Coordinate stovetop, oven, air fryer and microwave usage, including automatic preheating where needed.

Follow Cooking Mode

See what to do now, what is already running, countdowns, local notifications and timing adjustments.

Privacy

Designed to work offline.

CookSync does not require an account and does not use advertising, analytics, subscriptions or cloud synchronization. App data is stored locally on the device.
